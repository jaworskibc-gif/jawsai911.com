COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything prospects actually ask.

Short, direct answers about the platform, pricing, Compliance Lock™, migration, and what makes JAWSAI911 different.

What is Compliance Lock™?

A configurable compliance-control framework built into CCM's unified agent workspace. Agencies define their own required disclosures, verification steps, approval checkpoints, and enrollment conditions. The system prevents progression through designated workflow stages until those conditions are satisfied. Tested extensively with simulated AI-agent conversations with no known successful bypasses to date.

How is JAWSAI911 different from GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or Zoho?

Full feature parity with the leading all-in-one platforms at 15% less per month at every tier. On top of that: agentic AI workflow engine, multi-LLM orchestration via MCP, Compliance Lock™, custom AI agent builds, insurance-operations expertise, and multi-industry templates built in.

Who is this built for?

Insurance agencies (Medicare, ACA, final expense) as the primary focus. The platform also fits dealerships, barbershops and nail salons, real estate operators, and any local business that needs a working AI operations layer rather than a brochure site plus five disconnected SaaS tools.

Hammer Head ($252/mo): everything in Mako plus unlimited sub-accounts, phone/email rebill at cost, full API access, multi-LLM orchestration, custom automation builder, Shark SuperSite deployment, dedicated onboarding.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14-day free trial at every tier. No setup fee. No long-term contract required.

Can you migrate my current platform?

Yes. Great White tier includes white-glove migration from GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, or other common stacks. Hammer Head tier includes self-serve migration tools plus dedicated onboarding support.

How long does setup take?

Self-serve setup on Mako: same day. Hammer Head with dedicated onboarding: typically 3-5 business days. Great White with white-glove migration: typically 7-14 business days depending on source platform complexity.

Can I keep my existing phone number and email?

Yes. We port existing numbers and integrate with your current email domain. Phone and email usage is billed at cost on Hammer Head and Great White (no markup), passed through transparently on Mako.

Do you build custom AI agents?

Yes. Great White tier includes one custom AI agent build per contract. Additional agents and scoped custom development projects are available on request. What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel at any time with no termination fees. We provide a full export of your contacts, pipelines, call recordings, and data in standard formats (CSV, JSON) within 30 days of cancellation. No lock-in.

Do you handle HIPAA or regulatory compliance?

Enterprise and Great White tiers include infrastructure configured for HIPAA-aware workflows. Compliance Lock™ workflow controls support your compliance oversight responsibilities but do not eliminate regulatory risk or replace licensed-agent responsibilities. We work with your compliance team to configure checkpoints matching your approved scripts and procedures.
