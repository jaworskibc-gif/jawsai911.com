PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Shark SuperSite

You're covered front to back. Our sites are double stacked. No two Smart Site stacks are exactly the same - each one is tailored to your industry, market, and goals.

How it works

From stranger to reviewed customer - no re-typing

Visitor lands on Layer 1

Sees real reviews, services, and a clear way to reach you: form, chat, or phone.

Contact drops straight into Layer 2

No manual entry. Form fill becomes a pipeline card. Missed call becomes a logged lead with auto text-back already sent.

Team works from one shared view

Call outcomes, follow-up dates, and invoices live in the same system - visible to whoever needs them.

Back end keeps working after the sale

Review request fires automatically. Their story can feed the content engine that keeps Layer 1 looking active.

The loop closes

New reviews and fresh content strengthen the exact public site that brought the lead in. Front and back feed each other.
