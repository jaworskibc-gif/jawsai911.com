# Clearview Cold Call Opening Script And Rebuttals

Use this for B2B outreach to Medicare brokerage owners, sales directors, compliance leaders, and operations leaders.

This is not a beneficiary sales script. It is a brokerage-to-brokerage outreach script for booking a workflow review or short demo.

## Core Offer

Clearview is:
- one screen replacing the fragmented enrollment stack
- real-time compliance enforcement during the call
- AI coaching and objection handling support
- faster agent ramp
- cleaner audit trail
- fewer missed or unbilled enrollments

Your goal on a cold call is not to explain the whole platform.

Your goal is:
- earn 20 to 30 seconds
- name a real operational pain
- give one concrete outcome
- ask for a short workflow review

## Positioning Line

`We help Medicare brokerages replace the 8 to 11-screen enrollment mess with one guided workflow that improves QA, speeds agent ramp, and makes compliance issues structurally harder to miss.`

## Best Opening Script

`Hi, this is Bryan Jaworski with Clearview. I know I’m calling you cold, so I’ll be brief.`

`We built a Medicare enrollment workflow platform for brokerages that are tired of agents bouncing between Sunfire, carrier portals, compliance references, and internal tools all on the same call.`

`The reason for the call is simple: we help teams tighten QA, reduce process drag, and get agents through enrollments on one screen instead of eight or ten.`

`If I gave you a 20-second version of how it works, would you be open to hearing it?`

## 20-Second Version

`Clearview sits above the stack your agents already use. It connects the enrollment workflow, flags compliance issues in real time, surfaces the required language when needed, logs the interaction automatically, and gives agents live guidance instead of forcing managers to clean everything up later.`

`Teams look at us when they want better QA, faster onboarding, and fewer revenue leaks from broken process.`

## Best Transition To Meeting

`If that’s even mildly relevant, the next step is not a big pitch. It’s a short workflow review so I can see how your agents move through enrollments today and show you where Clearview would remove friction.`

`Would a 15-minute review next week be unreasonable?`

## Stronger Direct Ask

Use this if the person sounds sharp and busy.

`I’m not asking you to buy anything on this call. I’m asking for 15 minutes to compare your current enrollment workflow against what we built and see if there’s a real fit.`

`I’m trying to reach the person who owns sales operations, compliance workflow, or agent onboarding for the Medicare side.`

`This is about reducing QA drag and streamlining live enrollments, not a consumer pitch. Who is the best person for that?`

If asked what it is:

`It’s a workflow platform for Medicare brokerages that consolidates the live enrollment process, compliance guidance, and agent coaching into one operating layer.`

## Discovery Questions

Use only 2 to 4 of these on a cold call. Do not interrogate.

- `How many systems are your agents touching during a normal enrollment?`
- `Where do QA issues usually show up today?`
- `How much manager time goes into compliance cleanup after the fact?`
- `How long does it take a new agent to become truly productive?`
- `How are you handling objection coaching on live calls right now?`
- `Where do enrollments or revenue slip through the cracks operationally?`
- `If you could remove one point of friction from your current workflow tomorrow, what would it be?`

## Rebuttal Sheet

### 1. `We already have Sunfire / EnrollHere / carrier portals.`

`That makes sense. We are not replacing the fact that you use those systems. We sit above them so the agent is not manually bouncing across them without guidance.`

`The issue usually isn’t whether a brokerage has tools. It’s whether those tools operate like one workflow or a pile of tabs.`

### 2. `We already have a CRM / dialer / QA team.`

`Right, and Clearview is not a generic CRM pitch. It addresses the live enrollment workflow itself: real-time guidance, real-time compliance handling, and one-screen execution.`

`Most teams already have software. The gap is orchestration during the call.`

### 3. `We’re good.`

`Understood. Usually when someone says that, it means one of two things: either the workflow really is tight, or nobody has shown you a better operating model yet.`

`Would it be unreasonable to spend 15 minutes pressure-testing that assumption? If there’s no gap, I’ll tell you.`

### 4. `Just send me information.`

`Happy to. I can send something, but usually decks get ignored without context.`

`If I send the one-pager, would you be open to a 12-minute follow-up after you’ve seen it?`

### 5. `We’re not looking right now.`

`That’s fair. Most groups are not actively shopping until pain forces it.`

`Out of curiosity, when you eventually do revisit workflow or QA infrastructure, what usually triggers that conversation on your side?`

### 6. `We don’t have budget.`

`Understood. That’s exactly why I frame this around operational return, not software spend.`

`If a platform improves QA, shortens ramp time, and helps prevent broken enrollments from leaking revenue, the real question is whether the cost of the current process is already higher than the cost of fixing it.`

`Worth a quick look, or should I circle back in a different quarter?`

### 7. `We built something internally.`

`That’s actually useful to know. Internal builds usually mean the pain is real.`

`The question then becomes: is your internal tool just storing process, or is it actively guiding agents live, enforcing compliance moments, and reducing manager cleanup?`

### 8. `Our agents already know what they’re doing.`

`Strong agents still work inside weak systems.`

`The best teams use infrastructure to make top performers more consistent and average performers less risky. This is about system quality, not insulting the agents.`

### 9. `Compliance is handled by management.`

`Exactly, and that’s the expensive part.`

`We’re trying to move compliance support closer to the moment of execution so managers spend less time cleaning up and more time improving performance.`

### 10. `We don’t want AI on live calls.`

`That’s a healthy concern. The value is not “AI for AI’s sake.”`

`The value is guided execution: surfacing the right prompt, the right compliance language, and the right next step at the right time.`

`If the label creates resistance, think of it as workflow intelligence inside the live enrollment process.`

### 11. `This sounds complicated.`

`It would be a problem if it made the agent’s day more complicated.`

`The whole point is the opposite: fewer screens, fewer misses, less memory burden, less cleanup.`

### 12. `Call me in a few months.`

`Fair enough. Before I do, what would need to be true for that conversation to actually matter when I call back?`

`More agents? AEP prep? QA pressure? Carrier growth?`

## Best Short Rebuttals

Use these when you need to stay fast.

- `You already have tools. I’m talking about making them operate like one workflow.`
- `This is not CRM software. This is live enrollment infrastructure.`
- `I’m not asking for a buying decision. I’m asking for a short workflow review.`
- `If there’s no gap, we’ll know quickly.`
- `The pain usually isn’t lack of software. It’s fragmented execution.`

## Best Call Flow

1. Permission-based opener
2. One operational pain point
3. One concrete outcome
4. Ask one diagnostic question
5. Move to a 15-minute workflow review

## Tone Rules

- sound calm, not hyped
- do not over-explain the product on the first call
- use operational language, not startup language
- ask short questions
- do not argue with objections
- convert resistance into curiosity

## What To Avoid

- `revolutionary`
- `game changer`
- `cutting edge`
- talking about AI before talking about workflow pain
- leading with too many features
- sounding like you are pitching beneficiaries instead of brokerage leaders
