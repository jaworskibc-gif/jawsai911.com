Automations We've Built
AI-assisted enrollment capabilities

Enrollment tools are designed to support agent guidance, structured information capture, call review, and manager oversight. Workflow controls support compliance responsibilities without eliminating regulatory risk.

① Lead Intake & Routing

New leads are captured, de-duplicated, scored, and routed to the right agent automatically — no manual list-pulling or copy-paste between systems.

② Real-Time Agent Assist

AI listens to the conversation and surfaces the right plan details, rebuttals, and next questions on screen, live, to support the agent.

③ Compliance Lock™ Workflow Controls

The system is designed to prevent progression through designated enrollment or workflow stages until their configured conditions are satisfied.

④ Auto Call Summaries → CRM

Every call is transcribed, summarized, and logged to the prospect's record with outcome, objections, and next steps — written for you.

⑤ Follow-Up & Reminder Engine

Callbacks, SEP/AEP deadlines, and follow-ups are scheduled automatically and pushed to agents to support timely follow-up.

⑥ Manager Command Center

Live dashboards roll up pipeline, agent activity, compliance risk, and conversion so managers see status and act in one view.
