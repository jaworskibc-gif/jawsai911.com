Compliance Lock™
The screen turns red. The call cannot move forward. Until it is compliant.

Every other platform records violations and reports them weeks later. Ours stops the call in the moment, shows the agent the exact required language, and stays locked until they say it. Procedural violations do not get flagged. They get prevented.

Your recordings become your defense, not your exposure

CMS requires every enrollment call recorded and stored for 10 years. On other systems that tape is evidence against you. On ours, it captures the correction happening in real time, a self-correcting system, on the record, every call.

Secret-shopper proof, by architecture

An agent cannot talk past a skipped SOA, missed TPMO disclaimer, or absent attestation. The workflow physically will not advance. One agent's mistake can put an entire agency's marketing authority at risk, unless the mistake cannot happen.

Every lock is a live coaching rep

The agent sees the exact compliant language, says it, and moves on. Paired with live coaching, violations become training data, caught by AI in the moment, corrected by humans for good.

Compliance Lock enforces procedural compliance steps like SOA capture, required disclosures, attestation, and active recording at the workflow level and provides real-time prompts for required verbal language. All calls remain fully recorded and auditable in accordance with CMS requirements. Compliance Lock supports and does not replace your organization's compliance program.
