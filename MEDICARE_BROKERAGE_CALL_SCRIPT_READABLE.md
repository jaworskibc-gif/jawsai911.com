# Medicare Brokerage Call Script

Use this for cold outreach to:
- owners
- founders
- sales directors
- operations leaders
- compliance leaders
- training leaders

This version avoids product-name issues and keeps the language simple.

## Core Positioning

`We help Medicare brokerages reduce workflow drag, improve QA consistency, and ramp agents faster through a guided enrollment system.`

## Best Opener

`Hi, this is Bryan Jaworski. I know I’m calling you cold, so I’ll be brief.`

`We built a guided enrollment system for Medicare brokerages that want less workflow friction during live enrollments, less manager cleanup, and better QA consistency.`

`If I gave you the 20-second version, would you be open to hearing it?`

## 20-Second Version

`Our system helps agents work through enrollments in a more guided way, reduces process drag, and makes it easier for teams to keep quality and execution consistent as they scale.`

`The reason companies talk to us is usually one of three things: too many screens, too much operational friction, or too much manager dependency during live enrollments.`

## Best Transition

`If that’s even mildly relevant, the next step is not a big pitch. It’s a short 15-minute workflow review so I can understand your current process and show you where our system may or may not fit.`

`Would 15 minutes next week be unreasonable?`

## Discovery Questions

- `How many systems are your agents typically touching during a live enrollment?`
- `Where does process friction usually show up for your team today?`
- `Where do QA issues tend to come from right now?`
- `How long does it take a new agent to become productive?`
- `How much manager involvement is needed to keep live enrollments moving cleanly?`

## Voicemail

`Hi, this is Bryan Jaworski. I’m reaching out because we built a guided enrollment system for Medicare brokerages that want less workflow drag, better QA consistency, and faster agent ramp. If that’s relevant, call me back at [your number]. Again, Bryan at [your number].`

## Gatekeeper Script

`I’m trying to reach the person who owns sales operations, workflow, QA, compliance process, or onboarding for the Medicare side. Who would be best for that?`

## Best CTA

`15-minute workflow review`

Do not use:
- strategy session
- consultation
- demo call

`workflow review` is lower friction and easier to book.
