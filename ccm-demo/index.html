A CORE CCM CAPABILITY

Compliance Lock™

Configurable Compliance Enforcement

Compliance Lock™ is a configurable compliance-control framework integrated directly into CCM’s unified agent workspace. Agencies can define the required disclosures, verification steps, approval checkpoints, and enrollment conditions for their own workflows.

There is no fixed number of gates. The number, order, and conditions of checkpoints are determined by each agency’s approved scripts and operational requirements.

Designated enrollment or workflow stages can be restricted until their configured requirements are satisfied. These controls support compliance oversight; they do not eliminate regulatory risk or replace licensed-agent responsibilities.
