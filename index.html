Compliance Lock™
Configurable Compliance Enforcement

One Screen. One Conversation. Compliance Built Into the Workflow.

Call review & scorecards

Historical call review and performance scorecards to support quality assurance and coaching.

Compliance Lock™ is a configurable compliance-control framework integrated directly into CCM's unified agent workspace. Agencies can define the required disclosures, verification steps, approval checkpoints, and enrollment conditions for their own workflows.

There is no fixed gate limit. The number and sequence of checkpoints depend on the agency's approved script and configuration. The system is designed to prevent progression through designated workflow stages until their required conditions are satisfied.

The system has undergone extensive AI-agent testing, including simulated call interactions, with no known successful bypasses observed to date. Workflow controls support compliance oversight but do not eliminate regulatory risk or replace licensed-agent responsibilities.
