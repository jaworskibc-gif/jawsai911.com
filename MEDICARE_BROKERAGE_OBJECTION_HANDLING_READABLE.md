# Medicare Brokerage Objection Handling

This version is plain, readable, and does not use the old product name.

## 1. `We already have our systems.`

`That makes sense. Usually the issue isn’t whether a brokerage has systems. It’s whether those systems operate like one clean workflow or whether agents are still bouncing between tabs, tools, and manager help during live enrollments.`

## 2. `We already have a CRM / QA team / compliance people.`

`Right. This is not a CRM replacement pitch. It’s about making the live enrollment workflow easier to run and easier to control as the team scales.`

## 3. `We’re good.`

`Understood. Usually that means either the workflow is genuinely tight, or nobody has shown you a better operating model yet.`

`Would it be unreasonable to spend 15 minutes pressure-testing that?`

## 4. `Just send me information.`

`Happy to. I can send something over.`

`If I do, would you be open to a short follow-up after you’ve had a chance to look at it?`

## 5. `We’re not looking right now.`

`That’s fair. Out of curiosity, when this kind of conversation does become relevant on your side, what usually triggers it? QA pressure, growth, onboarding issues, or workflow friction?`

## 6. `We don’t have budget.`

`Understood. That’s exactly why I frame this around operational return. If the current workflow is creating drag, inconsistency, or too much manager involvement, there’s already a cost there.`

## 7. `We built something internally.`

`That’s actually useful to know. It usually means the pain is real.`

`The question becomes whether the internal system is just supporting process on paper, or whether it’s actually making live enrollments cleaner and easier to run at scale.`

## 8. `Our agents already know what they’re doing.`

`Strong agents still benefit from stronger systems. This is not about insulting the agents. It’s about making execution more consistent and reducing operational drag around them.`

## 9. `Compliance is handled by management.`

`Exactly, and that’s often where the cost shows up. A lot of teams end up leaning too hard on managers to keep live execution clean.`

## 10. `This sounds complicated.`

`It would be a problem if it made the agent’s day more complicated. The whole point is the opposite: cleaner execution, less friction, and less dependency on manual workarounds.`

## 11. `Call me later.`

`Happy to. Before I do, what should be true for the conversation to be relevant when I call back?`

## Best Short Rebuttals

- `The issue usually isn’t lack of software. It’s fragmented execution.`
- `This is about workflow quality, not just adding another tool.`
- `I’m not asking for a buying decision. I’m asking for a short workflow review.`
- `If there’s no real fit, we’ll know quickly.`
- `The goal is less friction, not more complexity.`
