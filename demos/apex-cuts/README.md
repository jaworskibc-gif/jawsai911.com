## How to demo (sales walkthrough)

1. Open **`../../salon-supersite.html`** — product pitch + special offer.
2. Open **`index.html`** — mobile sticky Call / Text / Book, AI chat, reviews, stylist chairs, schema.
3. Submit the booking form — lead is saved to `localStorage` (`apex_leads`).
4. Open **`command.html`** — lead appears in Pipeline; walk Review Engine + 5 Lanes + GBP checklist.
